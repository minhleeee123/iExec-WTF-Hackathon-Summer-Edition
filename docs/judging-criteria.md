## 3. Product Interpretation — Not Official Wording

| Criterion | What Judges May Need to See | What the Product Must Prove | Evidence to Prepare | Demo Approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creativity | A new use case rather than a superficial wrapper | A distinctive privacy improvement in the workflow | Short explanation and comparison | Show the unique flow within one minute |
| End-to-end accessibility | No mocks and a complete working path | A stable demo journey | Live deployment and smoke test | Walk through the complete user journey |
| Technical implementation | Nox in the core flow | Nox handles privacy and composability | Code paths and network/deployment evidence | Show where Nox changes the workflow |
| UX | Clear and practical interaction | An understandable flow with few steps | Screens, interactions, and error handling | Narrate the user experience concisely |

## 4. Idea Validation Questions

- Does the idea directly address the challenge?
- Is the web/mobile app a core product rather than a cosmetic wrapper?
- Does mandatory technology play a real role in the core flow?
- Can the differentiator be explained in one sentence?
- Can the value be demonstrated within the time limit?
- Is there clear evidence for every criterion?
- Can the scope be completed before the deadline?

## 5. Point-Loss Risks

| Risk | Affected Criterion | Source or Basis | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mock data or an incomplete demo | End-to-end accessibility | SRC-001 | Use real data and a real workflow; add smoke tests |
| Nox is only attached superficially | Technical implementation | SRC-001 | Put Nox in the product's core flow |
| Video is too long or unclear | Video requirement | SRC-001 | Keep the video under four minutes and focus on the core flow |
